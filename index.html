Deriv Direct Connection

This direct connection mode connects your browser securely straight to Deriv's official WebSocket gateway without requiring a separate backend server.

Quick Setup Guide

  1. Go to your Deriv account settings and generate an **API Token** (ensure 'Read' and 'Trade' permissions are enabled).
  2. Go to the **Account** tab in this app.
  3. Paste your API Token and click **Connect WebSocket**.
  4. Verify that your balance displays correctly and ticks are streaming on the Home screen.
  5. Arm the bot using **Start Trading**.
